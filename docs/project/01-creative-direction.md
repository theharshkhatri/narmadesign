## Brand

NARMA.DESIGN is an independent graphic design and creative studio targeting Indian and international clients.

The experience is light-first, typography-led, geometry-driven, and editorial. It should feel like a continuous visual composition rather than unrelated sections.

## Homepage composition

1. **Navigation and brand entrance** — official logo, Work, Studio, Contact, and a compact project CTA. Keep navigation usable without scroll-triggered effects.
2. **Hero** — oversized multi-line typography, original geometric SVG artwork, a short description, and Explore Work / Start a Project actions. Reveal progressively without delaying access to content.
3. **Selected work** — three to six typed projects, varied ratios, asymmetrical arrangement, project metadata, accessible links, and a clear distinction between concept and commissioned work.
4. **Signature visual experiment** — original SVG paths and geometric transformations with one primary interaction and an attractive static fallback.
5. **Services and approach** — brand identity and visual systems; graphic and campaign design; art direction and visual exploration; a concise discovery-to-delivery process.
6. **Capabilities map** — a scannable, illustrative list of graphic design categories and common deliverables, clearly separated from confirmed project scope.
7. **Closing CTA and footer** — expressive closing statement, Start a Project, direct email, valid navigation/social links where applicable, and copyright.

Do not add invented testimonials, awards, client logos, or unsupported performance claims.

## Scope

The initial prototype focuses on the homepage, shared navigation/footer, selected work, at least one representative case study, studio content, and contact experience. CMS/database infrastructure, client accounts, blog workflow, complex filtering, large-scale video backgrounds, and unselected third-party integrations are outside the initial scope.
